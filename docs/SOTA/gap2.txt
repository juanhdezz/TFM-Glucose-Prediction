GAP 2 — Demographic balancing (sex or age) applied to physiological time-series windows

The CGM racial-disparity paper is the only clearly relevant hit I found that directly addresses demographic subgroup performance in a physiological time-series prediction setting. It does not use SMOTE-style balancing; it studies how changing the racial composition of training data affects performance and shows the gap can be reduced by personalization/transfer learning.
